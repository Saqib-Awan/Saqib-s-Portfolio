# Multimodal Audio and Speech AI Projects

Welcome to the **Multimodal Audio and Speech AI Projects** repository. This domain contains 11 advanced speech processing, voice AI, and acoustic machine listening implementations covering real-time voice streaming, cross-lingual voice cloning, audio forensics, and speech biomarkers.

## Domain Overview

This collection covers:
- Ultra-low latency conversational voice AI agents (Silero VAD, Whisper Turbo)
- Real-time speech-to-speech translation with speaker timbre cloning (NLLB-200)
- Industrial acoustic anomaly detection and machine listening (Log-Mel Conformer)
- Multi-speaker neural diarization and transcript attribution (PyAnnote 3.1)
- Speech emotion recognition and customer sentiment call analyzers (Wav2Vec 2.0)
- Automatic music demixing and 4-stem vocal separation (HTDemucs v4)
- Expressive neural text-to-speech with pitch and prosody control (VITS)
- Low-latency noise suppression and speech enhancement (DeepFilterNet v3)
- Audio deepfake and voice clone verification forensics (RawNet2, Bispectral)
- Low-power TinyML keyword spotting and wake-word detection (Micro-Conformer)
- Clinical speech biomarker screeners for mild cognitive decline

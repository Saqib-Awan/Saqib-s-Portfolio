# Customer Shopping Trends & Basket Analysis

**Author:** Saqib  

## Overview

This project analyses customer shopping behaviour to uncover spending patterns, category preferences, seasonal trends, and the impact of discounts and subscriptions. The insights help retailers personalise offers, optimise promotions, and improve customer retention.

## Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle – Customer Shopping Trends Dataset  
- **Rows:** 3,900 transactions  
- **Columns:** Customer ID, Age, Gender, Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Discount Applied, Subscription Status, Payment Method, etc.

## Visualisations Included

1. Purchase amount distribution (histogram + KDE)  
2. Average spending by category (horizontal bar)  
3. Seasonal spending trends (bar chart)  
4. Gender vs purchase amount (box plot)  
5. Age group vs purchase amount (box plot)  
6. Discount vs purchase amount (box plot)  
7. Subscription status impact (bar chart)  
8. Correlation heatmap  
9. Top 10 most purchased items (horizontal bar)  
10. Payment method preference (pie chart)

## How to Run

1. Open the notebook in Google Colab.  
2. Run all cells sequentially.  
3. The dataset will be downloaded automatically from GitHub.

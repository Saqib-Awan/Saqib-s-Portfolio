# Data Analysis Projects

A collection of applied statistical profiling, exploratory data analysis (EDA), predictive analytics, and econometrics projects developed by Muhammad Saqib. The repositories cover global financial market liquidity risk, randomized clinical trial biostatistics, geospatial traffic accident profiling, customer churn, e-commerce RFM segmentation, and organizational HR analytics. Each project includes complete code, datasets, and detailed methodologies.

## Project Index

1. `Airbnb Price Analysis (NYC 2019)/` - Geospatial and demographic exploratory analysis of short-term rental listings across New York City.
2. `Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis – Predicting Term Deposit Subscription/` - Conversion rate analysis and predictive modeling for bank marketing contact lists.
3. `COVID-19 Global Trends Analysis (Time Series & Geospatial)/` - Epidemiological transmission curves, case fatality rates, and choropleth mapping.
4. `Customer Churn Prediction & Exploratory Analysis/` - Telecommunication customer attrition diagnostics, tenure distributions, and service factor correlation.
5. `Customer Shopping Trends & Basket Analysis/` - Association rule mining (Apriori) and retail consumer purchasing patterns.
6. `E‑commerce Sales Analysis (RFM Segmentation & CLV)/` - Transactional customer stratification using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics.
7. `Global Financial Market Liquidity and Volatility Risk Analytics/` - Quantitative risk modeling, Monte Carlo Value-at-Risk (VaR), and macroeconomic crisis stress simulations.
8. `Global Superstore Sales Analysis/` - Multi-region commercial profitability, product category margins, and supply chain logistics analysis.
9. `Healthcare Clinical Trials and Epidemiological Analytics Suite/` - Biostatistical survival analysis (Kaplan-Meier), hazard ratios, and adverse event auditing.
10. `HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance/` - Organizational workforce turnover modeling and employee satisfaction analytics.
11. `Movie Industry Analysis (TMDB 5000 Movie Dataset)/` - Box office revenue drivers, production budget elasticity, and audience ratings analysis.
12. `US Accidents Analysis (2016-2023)/` - Large-scale spatial-temporal analysis of road traffic incidents across contiguous US states.
13. `Wine Quality Prediction – EDA & Classification/` - Physicochemical property correlation and classification modeling for viticultural quality.

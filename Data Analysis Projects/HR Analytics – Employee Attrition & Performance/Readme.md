# HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance

**Author:** Saqib  

## Overview

This project analyses employee attrition (turnover) using IBM’s HR dataset. It identifies key factors associated with employees leaving, such as monthly income, overtime, job satisfaction, and tenure. The insights help HR departments design targeted retention strategies.

## Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle – IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance  
- **Rows:** 1,470 employees  
- **Columns:** Attrition, Age, MonthlyIncome, JobSatisfaction, WorkLifeBalance, YearsAtCompany, Department, OverTime, MaritalStatus, etc.

## Visualisations Included

1. Attrition rate overall and by department  
2. Monthly income distribution by attrition (box plot)  
3. Age distribution by attrition (histogram + KDE)  
4. Job satisfaction vs attrition (stacked bar)  
5. Years at company by attrition (box plot)  
6. Overtime impact on attrition (bar chart)  
7. Marital status vs attrition (stacked bar)  
8. Correlation heatmap  
9. Feature importance from logistic regression  
10. Department-wise attrition percentage (horizontal bar)

## How to Run

1. Open the notebook in Google Colab.  
2. Run all cells sequentially.  
3. The dataset will be downloaded automatically (or upload manually if the URL fails).

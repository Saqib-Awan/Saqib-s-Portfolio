# Reinforcement Learning and Decision Intelligence Projects

Welcome to the **Reinforcement Learning and Decision Intelligence Projects** repository. This domain contains 11 production-grade reinforcement learning, sequential decision-making, and policy optimization implementations spanning algorithmic finance, autonomous driving, grid demand-response, AlphaZero board game mastery, and human preference alignment.

## Domain Overview

This collection covers:
- Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and RLHF alignment pipelines
- Algorithmic portfolio trading agents with continuous PPO and transaction cost penalties
- Autonomous driving policies in CARLA Simulator with Bird's-Eye-View (BEV) perception
- Dynamic pricing and yield optimization agents via Contextual Bandits and DQN
- Energy grid demand-response battery dispatchers with Dueling Double-DQN (D3QN)
- Multi-echelon supply chain replenishment agents (MADDPG) mitigating bullwhip effects
- AlphaZero chess and board game engines with Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)
- High sample-efficient continuous robotics control with Soft Actor-Critic (SAC)
- Competitive multi-agent swarm simulations with PettingZoo and QMIX value factorization
- Multi-agent traffic signal coordination with Eclipse SUMO and green wave synchronization
- Adaptive game AI bots combining Hierarchical Behavior Trees and dynamic difficulty adjustment

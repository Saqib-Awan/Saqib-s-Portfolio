# Autonomous AI Agents

A collection of autonomous multi-agent engineering implementations developed by Muhammad Saqib. The repositories feature hierarchical role-based swarms, self-correcting feedback loops, shared blackboard state graphs, tool-calling execution, and multi-domain reasoning across software engineering, quantitative finance, SOC threat hunting, clinical medicine, legal compliance, supply chain logistics, scientific discovery, AutoML, and cloud FinOps. Each project includes modular multi-file source code, dependencies, architecture diagrams, and comprehensive documentation.

## Project Index

1. `Autonomous Clinical Diagnosis and Treatment Plan Verification Agent/` - Multi-specialty medical agent panel cross-verifying differential diagnoses, drug interactions, and clinical guidelines.
2. `Autonomous Cybersecurity Incident Response & Threat Hunting Agent/` - Autonomous SOC hunting swarm triaging SIEM telemetry, mapping MITRE ATT&CK vectors, and deploying automated containment rules.
3. `Autonomous Customer Support and Resolution Swarm with Escalation Gateways/` - Omnichannel customer resolution swarm tracking emotional sentiment, solving inquiries, and routing retention escalations.
4. `Autonomous Data Science AutoML Pipeline Generator Agent/` - End-to-end data science agent automating exploratory analysis, feature synthesis, multi-algorithm benchmarking, and training script export.
5. `Autonomous Devops Cloud Infrastructure and Cost Optimization Agent/` - Autonomous FinOps and SRE agent scanning multi-cloud infrastructure, pruning orphaned resources, and submitting Terraform pull requests.
6. `Autonomous E-Commerce Supply Chain Inventory Reordering Swarm/` - Multi-agent logistics swarm forecasting demand surges, comparing supplier price schedules, and transmitting purchase orders.
7. `Autonomous Financial Market Researcher & Due-Diligence Agent/` - Institutional equity research agent ingesting SEC EDGAR filings, computing DCF models, and generating investment memorandums.
8. `Autonomous Legal Contract Review and Compliance Audit Agent/` - Commercial contract intelligence swarm extracting clauses, auditing unlimited liability risks, and authoring compliant redline amendments.
9. `Autonomous Multimodal Content Creator and Brand Marketing Squad/` - Coordinated creative squad orchestrating brand strategists, copywriters, SEO auditors, and visual directors to build multi-platform campaigns.
10. `Autonomous Scientific Literature Review and Hypothesis Generator/` - Bio-informatics research agent mining preprint literature, detecting empirical contradictions, and designing testable experimental assays.
11. `Multi-Agent Software Engineering Squad/` - Hierarchical software development swarm coordinating Product Owner, Systems Architect, Coder, and QA Reviewer roles with AST validation and automated unit testing.
